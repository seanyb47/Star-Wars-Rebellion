# Peoples Art Guide v1 — reference copy

**Not a canon source.** `CANON.md` §10 says so explicitly: *"It treats the world
bible as canon. Its Reef-folk section is superseded by section 5A; its other
peoples' traits are proposals until Sean confirms them."* Kept here because the
production material in it — heights, palettes, silhouette rules, the
do-not-drift lists and the cross-people checks — is genuinely useful to an
artist, and because `docs/ship-art-direction.md` leans on it for the crews.

**Three of its "canon foundation" lines are lore that v4.0 cut**, and they are
wrong wherever they appear below:

| It says | v4.0 says |
|---|---|
| Reef-folk *"were once enslaved as Imperium oarsmen"* | **Rejected.** §12: the Crown keeps no slaves. |
| The Hushed come *"from the half drowned islands of the Drowned Reach"* | **Cut.** The Drowned Reach no longer exists; their origin is open question §11.1. |
| *"Supernatural teal is reserved for the Deep"* | **Retired.** The Deep as a system is gone (§5). |

Everything else stands as proposals. Sean supplied this copy on 19 September;
the text below is the document verbatim, converted from the .docx.

Master of the Seven Seas Peoples Art Guide
Crew encyclopedia visual development draft 1

Maren Quist  Reef folk

Anselm Big Torvik  Urskin

Purpose  Give artists a repeatable anatomy palette costume and portrait system for every people currently represented in the crew encyclopedia.
Status  Canon traits are preserved. Measurements palettes and expanded male and female variants are proposed for review.

How to use this guide
This guide converts the current crew portraits and world bible descriptions into production rules. Use the canon line as the nonnegotiable identity. Treat every field marked proposed as a starting point that can be edited without changing established lore.
Shared portrait language
Stylized cinematic pirate adventure illustration with painterly surfaces and believable late seventeenth to eighteenth century maritime clothing.
Head and shoulders or upper torso in a square crop. Keep the face and defining silhouette readable at 96 pixels.
Natural daylight or warm maritime light. Supernatural teal is reserved for the Deep and should not become a universal rim light.
One person only. No text borders interface or modern objects. Avoid photorealism chibi steampunk generic high fantasy and excessive cartooning.
Faction is shown through clothing construction and upkeep. Crown is ordered fitted maintained and green brass ivory. Confederacy is individualized repaired weathered and crimson black cream.

Humans

Adaira Hale  current human portrait
IDENTITY TESTCould an artist redraw this person in silhouette and still make the people recognizable at game scale?
Canon foundation
Humans live throughout the Seven Seas and form most of both major fleets. The Rumor Guild is human rather than a separate species.
Production anatomy
Silhouette  The baseline for scale and proportion. Distinction comes from island ancestry age occupation wealth weather exposure and faction rather than a single human look.
Body range  Keep the full believable range of human bodies. Sailors develop rope and oar strength sun damage scars and practical hair. Officers may be softer or older without looking incapable.
Male and female range  Show men women and gender diverse sailors across every role. Do not make women cleaner younger or less weathered by default. Use the same occupational logic for clothing and scars.
Proposed palette
Salt cream#D9D0BA
Sun brown#9B674C
Deep umber#4D2F28
Imperial green#264B3D
Free crimson#8C3232

Clothing and material language
Crown humans wear fitted coats ordered collars polished brass and controlled repetition. Confederate humans wear layered pieces local dyes repairs personal tokens and mixed provenance.
Do not drift into
A single real world ethnicity standing in for a faction; pristine pirates; villain coding through skin tone; modern hair and cosmetics; costume party excess.

Reef folk

Maren Quist  current Reef folk portrait
IDENTITY TESTCould an artist redraw this person in silhouette and still make the people recognizable at game scale?
Canon foundation
Amphibious people of Coralhome. They have gill slits at the throat luminous eyes and skin that shifts color with mood. They grow ships from living coral and are famed admirals. They were once enslaved as Imperium oarsmen.
Production anatomy
Silhouette  Tall poised amphibious humanoids. A backward swept fin or coral like cranial crest creates the silhouette. The neck remains visible so the gill slits read.
Body range  Proposed range 5 ft 5 to 6 ft 7 and 120 to 240 lb. Long limbs swimmer shoulders powerful back and flexible rib cage. Hands have subtle webbing not flippers.
Male and female range  Dimorphism should be subtle. Any sex may have a tall or compact crest. Proposed cues are crest branching skin pattern and facial softness rather than human breasts eyelashes or extreme waist differences.
Proposed palette
Calm teal#3E8790
Thoughtful blue#396C86
Alarm coral#B65F54
Joy gold#D6B35C
Deep shadow#183D47

Clothing and material language
Open or split collars preserve gill function. Use shell buttons woven sea grass waterproof leather brass and living coral only for Confederacy aligned or cultural pieces.
Do not drift into
Mermaid tails; fish heads; exposed decorative gills with no anatomy; neon rainbow skin; heavy coral on every individual; copying one current female portrait as the only body type.

Bog folk

Tobias Renn  current Bog folk portrait
IDENTITY TESTCould an artist redraw this person in silhouette and still make the people recognizable at game scale?
Canon foundation
Wrinkled patient amphibious guerrillas from the Sea of Storms swamps. They excel at concealment endurance and battles arranged on their own terms.
Production anatomy
Silhouette  Human scale but unmistakable in profile through long folded facial planes shallow brow ridges close ears and rope like neck folds. Their posture is still observant and economical.
Body range  Proposed range 5 ft 5 to 6 ft 5 and 135 to 250 lb. Dense tendon and broad feet suit mud and water. Skin looks resilient and rain softened not diseased.
Male and female range  Both sexes share the folded amphibious facial structure. Vary jaw breadth cheek depth and build gently. Age and environmental wear should matter more than glamour cues.
Proposed palette
Peat olive#5D6646
Reed tan#A49368
Rain grey#778180
Mud umber#554336
Moss light#98A56F

Clothing and material language
Waxed cloth short cloaks wrapped boots reed camouflage dark leather and dull hardware. Gear is quiet snag resistant and repairable in a swamp.
Do not drift into
Orc tusks; zombie rot; comic frog anatomy; bright tropical green; random tribal shorthand; bulky armor that defeats stealth and amphibious movement.

Shoal folk

Wren Tally  current Shoal folk portrait
IDENTITY TESTCould an artist redraw this person in silhouette and still make the people recognizable at game scale?
Canon foundation
Small webbed night eyed people of the Shoals. They chatter quickly can hear a ship hull creak a mile away and are the finest watchers in the world.
Production anatomy
Silhouette  Compact body oversized dark eyes and broad sail like external ears. The ears and head read first even in a black silhouette. Hands and feet are lightly webbed.
Body range  Proposed range 4 ft 0 to 5 ft 2 and 60 to 120 lb. Light bones flexible ankles quick balance and a low center of gravity suit rigging and reefs.
Male and female range  Use shared species anatomy. Variation comes through ear shape markings facial breadth and build. Avoid turning females into childlike mascots or males into miniature humans.
Proposed palette
Shell beige#B9AA91
Night umber#3A332F
Reef rust#9A5B43
Eye black#17191B
Sea lavender#756C82

Clothing and material language
Close fitted rigging clothes ear safe head wraps soft boots compact knives listening tubes and tiny tool rolls. Keep hats clear of the ears unless specially cut.
Do not drift into
Child proportions; cute pet treatment; bat wings; goblin teeth; enormous unsupported ears; jewelry or helmets that would interfere with hearing.

Urskin

Anselm Big Torvik  current Urskin portrait

Brannoc Tull  current Urskin portrait
Canon foundation
Huge shaggy tusked sea bear people from Northreach. They are whalers and harpooners loyal to death slow to anger and terrifying beyond it.
Production anatomy
Silhouette  A head taller and roughly half again as broad as a human. Barrel chest long heavy arms high furred shoulders bear like muzzle and two readable lower tusks.
Body range  Proposed range 6 ft 8 to 8 ft 2 and 320 to 700 lb. Massive does not mean obese. Build weight through rib cage shoulder girdle forearms hands and cold weather insulation.
Male and female range  All sexes are huge shaggy and tusked. Proposed female Urskin average slightly shorter with a lower shoulder line and often smaller tusks but remain visibly stronger than most humans.
Proposed palette
Ice cream#D8D2C3
Seal grey#6E6B67
Walrus brown#70503C
Storm black#2A2B2D
North red#8F3E35

Clothing and material language
Heavy wool seal leather rope wraps whalebone toggles broad harnesses and harpoon gear. Clothing must be built to their scale with large closures and reinforced load points.
Do not drift into
Wookiee copying; gorilla posture; Viking horn helmets; cute teddy bear faces; human sized props; perfectly groomed fur; making every Urskin an enraged brute.

The Hushed

Sable  current Hushed portrait
IDENTITY TESTCould an artist redraw this person in silhouette and still make the people recognizable at game scale?
Canon foundation
Pale eel thin silent people from the half drowned islands of the Drowned Reach. An old bargain binds their people to the Crown though individuals may resist it. Nobody hears them coming.
Production anatomy
Silhouette  Tall narrow shoulders long neck fine hands and a face that reads as quiet rather than dead. A hood strengthens the vertical silhouette but is cultural not anatomical.
Body range  Proposed range 5 ft 7 to 6 ft 7 and 105 to 185 lb. Low body fat long flexible joints and smooth pale skin. Their movement is controlled heel light and almost ripple free.
Male and female range  Dimorphism is restrained. Both sexes can be severe delicate or muscular within the narrow frame. Avoid beauty versus monster coding; use jaw shape hand size and subtle build variation.
Proposed palette
Eel pearl#C8C8C1
Drowned blue#596B73
Silt violet#625968
Lamp black#202328
Ghost teal#5D9291

Clothing and material language
Matte waxed layers close cuffs soft soles narrow belts hidden fasteners and hoods shaped for rain. Crown service may add one precise green or brass token without making them parade soldiers.
Do not drift into
Undead makeup; vampire fangs; glowing eyes by default; black leather assassin cliché; skeletal starvation; supernatural smoke replacing real anatomy.

The Rumor Guild

Human proportion reference  Guild styling is cultural
IDENTITY TESTCould an artist redraw this person in silhouette and still make the people recognizable at game scale?
Canon foundation
The Rumor Guild is a human institution based around Bothaway not a biological people. Its members are ink stained sharp and willing to sell information to both sides.
Production anatomy
Silhouette  Human anatomy. Recognition comes from layers of pockets writing tools ledgers coded ribbons and the composed posture of someone who already knows the answer.
Body range  Use the full human range. Guild members may be dock runners archivists couriers tavern brokers or wealthy factors. Physical uniformity would weaken the concept.
Male and female range  Every gender participates at every rank. Keep presentation purposeful and locally adapted. Information work should not default to seduction for women or violence for men.
Proposed palette
Ink navy#253746
Ledger cream#D8CFB9
Wax red#8A3B38
Tarnished brass#92774A
Soot#30302F

Clothing and material language
Layered practical coats hidden pockets inked fingertips sealed notes small spectacles cipher wheels and one discreet guild mark. Quality ranges from dockside to courtly.
Do not drift into
Treating the Guild as a race; identical uniforms; spy movie gadgets; fetishized courtesans; overt faction colors that compromise neutrality.

Cross people consistency checks
Check
Pass condition
Scale
A human remains the visual ruler. Shoal folk read distinctly smaller. Urskin read larger even when portraits are cropped identically.
Faces
Species identity comes from bone soft tissue ears eyes gills fur and muzzle not from face paint alone.
Sex
Male and female variants share the same species anatomy. Secondary sex cues never erase the silhouette.
Faction
A people is not a faction except where canon says sworn. Culture remains visible beneath Crown or Confederate service.
Coral
Visible living coral belongs to Reef folk and the Confederacy only. It is never normal Crown construction or ornament.
Readability
At 96 pixels retain one dominant head shape one clear eye pattern and no more than two small cultural props.
Tone
Adventure first charm second darkness selectively. Nonhuman people are societies not monsters or mascots.
