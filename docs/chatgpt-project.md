## 1. Project instructions

```

THE STYLE LINE, pasted at the end of every prompt, word for word: cinematic stylized historical realism, hand-painted trading-card illustration, authentic late-17th/18th-century maritime detail, strong readable silhouette, dramatic natural lighting, textured painterly brushwork, rich colours, romantic adventure atmosphere, approximately 70% historical swashbuckling adventure, 20% nautical fantasy and mysticism, 10% whimsy; never photorealistic, steampunk, grimdark, chibi, childish, generic high fantasy, futuristic or excessively cartoonish

FRAMING, every image: chest-up, three-quarter angle facing slightly to the viewer's left, head and shoulders in the upper half, environment visible behind, vertical 4:5. No text, borders, watermark, logo, extra characters.

CHARACTER 1 — Secretary Sabine Marlow, human, sixty-one. A small woman, straight-backed, seated at a desk as if the chair were an afterthought. Iron-grey hair cropped short, no wig. A lined, intelligent, appealing face — forty-five years of reading dispatches by lamplight, and grey, level eyes amused at something she is not going to share. She wears the Admiralty's civil dress in the Imperium's colours: a deep sea-green coat with cream facings and a narrow gold edge at collar and cuff — no epaulettes, no braid, no sword, the uniform of the staff and not the fleet — a high white collar and a black stock, with ink on the first two fingers of the right hand. At her throat, one small brass clasp holding three plain mourning rings on a short chain. Half-moon reading spectacles in her hand, and a black cane with a plain brass head leaning against the desk. Crown Imperium — order, stability, trade, civilization, a brighter tomorrow: deep Imperial green, antique gold and brass, warm ivory, black iron and refined wood; civilized and impressive, never sinister; no coral anywhere. Behind her, through a tall window: clear blue sky, white stone, tall ships in line. Bright, warm, proud. Her coat is the same deep Imperial-green and warm ivory as the three reference portraits' uniforms; she must read as the same navy's staff.

CHARACTER 2 — Mr Pennywhistle, a big, battered sea-parrot, heavier than a parrot has any right to be, perched on a length of whalebone lashed to a stanchion. Plumage in the Confederacy's own patchwork: a rust-red body faded to coral-pink at the breast, a mantle of squid-ink blue-black across the shoulders, wing coverts that look dyed rather than grown, a ragged tail with two feathers missing. A big grey hooked beak with a chip out of the upper edge. One eye — the left — a hard yellow ring around a black pupil fixed on the viewer; over the right, a tiny square of leather stitched on with sailmaker's thread and finished with a bone button. A brass ring on one leg with three links of snapped chain hanging from it. Leaning forward off the perch, head cocked to bring the good eye round. Free Confederacy — freedom, opportunity, independence, brotherhood, a bolder tomorrow: crimson and rust red, black, weathered wood, brass, cream, patched and dyed sails; individual and improvised, never standardized, and never villainous. Behind him, close and quiet: a Confederacy quay — tarred timber, coiled rope, patched and dyed canvas, a brass lantern, painted planking, bunting in faded colour — NO white stone, NO castle, NO city on a hill, NO open sea horizon, NO flags flying, NO open flame: this is a pirate haven built of wood and canvas, nothing the Imperium would build. Bright, warm, mischievous.

THE THREE EXPRESSIONS. Neutral: as described. Grave — Marlow: spectacles off and in her hand, the amusement gone from the eyes, jaw set, nothing else moves; Pennywhistle: hunched and low, head down, feathers flat, good eye narrowed. Encouraged — Marlow: the barest dry near-smile, one eyebrow a fraction higher, the head lifted a degree; Pennywhistle: upright and bright, chest out, head cocked high, beak slightly open mid-squawk, feathers lifted.

BACKGROUNDS. The two advisors never share a background. Marlow's is the Admiralty: white stone and ships in line through a window. Pennywhistle's is a pirate quay of wood, rope and canvas, and no white stone appears anywhere in his frame. Backgrounds stay quiet — these stills will be animated and the background must be able to hold still: no flags, no open flame, no busy water.

RULES. The three reference portraits in "Narrators / style" are the anchors; every image matches their brushwork, palette, lighting and register. The first approved Marlow neutral then anchors her other two, the first approved Pennywhistle neutral his. When changing an expression, change only the face: pose, clothing, plumage, background and lighting stay identical. The signature details survive every variant: Marlow's three rings at the throat, the ink on two fingers, the spectacles and the cane; Pennywhistle's stitched leather patch with the bone button and the three links of snapped chain on his leg.

FILE NAMES, fixed, PNG, vertical 4:5, largest size available: marlow_neutral.png, marlow_grave.png, marlow_encouraged.png, pennywhistle_neutral.png, pennywhistle_grave.png, pennywhistle_encouraged.png.

DELIVERY: Google Drive, folder "7 Seas / Narrators / stills".
```
